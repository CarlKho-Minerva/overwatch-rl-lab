# Episode 1: Introduction to Reinforcement Learning

*Outline for one episode of a video series, "RL with Overwatch". Footage: the island runs in this repository.*

**Working title:** "The Bot Who Never Learns, and the Bot Who Already Knows"

**Cold open (first 10 seconds):** The Tracer named RANDOM WALK takes a few steps along Workshop Island's edge and runs off the drop.

**Beats:**
1. RANDOM WALK wandering the strip, then falling. "48 squares, four moves, and only rewards: minus one a step, minus a hundred for the drop."
2. Squares lighting up as she walks. "Everything she has seen is her history. Her state is just the square she stands on."
3. Q, ROW ONLY pacing up and down the edge row. "Give a bot too small a summary and two different places look the same."
4. Arrows on every square; PLANNER walks the edge row in eleven steps. "PLANNER was handed the rules. She never needed to fall."
5. Both score lines side by side. "Minus 112.7 on average for the bot who never learns, 39 for the one who knows. Every learner lives between."

**The experiment shown:** RANDOM WALK against PLANNER on the same strip, with their mean returns.

**Closing line:** "Next time: what makes a square a state, and how to work out what it is worth."

**The game for this episode:** [Game 1: No Coach](../workshop/games/g01.md). The viewer can load it and play along (`workshop/compiled/game-01.txt`).
