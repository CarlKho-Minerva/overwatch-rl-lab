*Outline for one episode of a video series, "RL with Overwatch". Footage: the island runs in this repository.*

**Title:** "The Blocker"
**Cold open (first 10 seconds):** a Tracer runs the edge row and stops dead against a standing enemy bot at column 6; cut to the name over each.
1. Ten bots crossing the empty strip, score lines rising. "For nine episodes the island never fought back."
2. A runner bumping the fixed blocker, then routing inland. "Against a blocker who never moves, this is the old problem: a best response."
3. Arrows on squares, "sees" beside "blind". "One runner is told where the blocker is. The other bumps to find out."
4. The learning blocker in the far inland row against a blind runner, then changing rows against a seeing one. "The blocker learns too, so the runner's world moves."
5. Score lines: -14.0, -46.1, -35.1, -66.4. "The learning blocker cost more every time, and knowing more did not help yet."
6. PLANNER walking her flawless line. "Silver's champions searched. Nobody on this island does."
**The experiment shown:** the two-player build, 12 short runs, labelled exploratory.
**Closing line:** "When the other side chooses too, the best move depends on whom you play."
