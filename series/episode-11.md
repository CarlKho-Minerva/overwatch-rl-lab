# Episode 11: The Wall She Cannot See

*Outline for the finale of a video series, "RL with Overwatch". Footage: the hidden-wall builds and the local-model runs in this repository. The numbers to put on screen are in `course/lesson-11-partial-observability.md`.*

**Working title:** The Wall She Cannot See

**Cold open (first 10 seconds):** A Tracer walks along the edge, stops dead at an empty-looking square, and walks at it again. And again. A red ball floats above the square. She cannot see it; you can.

**Beats:**
1. Three Tracers side by side, names over their heads: RUNNER BLIND, RUNNER REMEMBERS BUMPS, RUNNER SEES THE WALL. "Same rule, same rewards. The only difference is what each is told."
2. BLIND reaches the wall and keeps walking at it until the step counter runs out. "Her table has one line for this square. One line cannot say forward before the bump and sideways after it."
3. REMEMBERS bumps once, steps inland, walks round. "One remembered fact. That is the whole repair."
4. The score lines for the three, test crossings only. "More practice does not help the blind one. She is not short of practice."
5. Cut to a chat window: a support agent asks what the customer wants, looks the booking up, then asks again. "The same bug in a language model: shown only the latest message, it forgets the answer it just got."
6. Two runs of one real benchmark task, same model: zero, then one. "The first time, the plumbing dropped every tool result. The score blamed the model."

**The experiment shown:** BLIND against REMEMBERS BUMPS on the short strip (wall in any row), share of test crossings reaching the goal; then the four conditions of the hidden order on a local model.

**Closing line:** "Before you ask whether the agent is clever enough, ask what it was shown."
