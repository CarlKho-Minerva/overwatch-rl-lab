# Episode 9: Exploration and Exploitation

*Outline for one episode of a video series, "RL with Overwatch". Footage: the island runs in this repository.*

**Title:** "The price of finding out"
**Cold open:** ten Tracers on the strip; Q + OPTIMISM steps off the edge on purpose, and her score line drops by 100.
- Beat 1: Q-LEARNING mid-crossing, then a random step into the drop. "She knew the way. A coin made her fall."
- Beat 3: Q + OPTIMISM falling once at each edge square, then never. "Assume the best until proven otherwise; the proof costs one fall per square."
- Beat 4: Q + COUNT BONUS, arrows changing as visits add up. "A bonus for what you have barely tried, shrinking as you try it."
- Beat 5: score lines for all four over 150 episodes. "Falls per 100: 26.5 for the coin, about 7 for the optimists."
- Beat 6: MONTE CARLO + OPTIMISM stepping off again and again. "Optimism chooses what she tries. It cannot fix how she judges what she saw."
**The experiment shown:** the four Q explorers, falls per 100.
**Closing line:** "Exploring by uncertainty pays a fixed bill and stops. Exploring by dice never stops paying."
