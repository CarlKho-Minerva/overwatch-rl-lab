# Game 2: Mini or Mega
**Lecture 2:** Markov decision processes. **You play:** Tracer, who sets the discount and walks the row. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
A reward later is worth less than one now; the discount says how much less. Change it and the best choice changes. A pad's worth is one step's reward plus the discounted worth of the pad ahead.

## The Overwatch metaphor
The mini health pack close by against the mega across the map: take the small thing now, or walk for the big one. Worths 15 and 50 (75 and 250 health, divided by 5).

## How to play
Mini 3 pads behind the start, mega 10 ahead. Part 1, four rounds: F turns the dial to an untried setting (0.5, 0.8, 0.9, 1), R locks it. Click to predict her pack; the packs show worth x discount ^ pads and a bot Tracer walks to the larger. One question on lecture 1's state. Part 2: steps cost 1, discount 0.9. Pads fill from the mega backward; predict the one marked ? (melee is option three). Walk the row with WASD while the screen checks "this pad = -1 + 0.9 x the pad ahead" on each pad. Last: the start pad if she always walks to the mini.

## What to notice
The choice flips between 0.8 and 0.9 (the switch is near 0.84). The start pad is worth 10.92 walking to the mega and 8.23 walking to the mini; one pad back, the mini walk wins.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
Return: the discounted sum of rewards. Value: expected return from a state under a policy. Bellman expectation equation: a one-step look-ahead. The optimal policy depends on gamma.

## What it leaves out
No dice: every step lands where it aims. The lecture counts a pack k steps away as gamma^(k-1); here it is gamma^k, which changes no choice. In part 1 he chooses the dial order and predicts; the bot walks.

## Log lines
- `bot`: 1 if the bot exists
- `dial`: round, gamma, mini value, mega value, best (0 mini, 1 mega)
- `walked`: round, gamma, best, arrived
- `pad`: policy (0 to mega, 1 to mini), pad, value
- `walk`: pad, its value, value of the pad ahead
- `walkdone`: pads checked, rule held, reached the mega
- `end`: predictions right, made
