# Game 2: Mini or Mega
**Lecture 2:** Markov decision processes. **You play:** Tracer, who sets the discount and walks the row. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
The mega is bigger, but later, and a reward later is worth less than one now. A dial, the discount, sets how much less.

Turn that dial and the right pack can change.

A pad's worth is one step's reward plus the discounted worth of the pad ahead.

## The Overwatch metaphor
The mini health pack close by against the mega across the map: take the small thing now, or walk for the big one.
Worths 15 and 50 (75 and 250 health, divided by 5).

## How to play
Beside the lecture 2 page: read a stop, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits there. Mini 3 pads behind the start, mega 10 ahead.

1-4. One dial round each: F turns the dial to an untried setting (0.5, 0.8, 0.9, 1), R locks it, a click predicts her pack, a bot walks to the larger.

5. Lecture 1: is her pad enough to predict what comes next?
6. F fills the pads (step cost 1, discount 0.9); predict the ? pad; walk the row with WASD while the screen checks each pad.
7. The start pad if she always walks to the mini: more or less?
8. F for the debrief.

## What to notice
Between 0.8 and 0.9 the choice flips. Same packs. Only the dial moved.

The start pad is worth 10.92 walking to the mega and 8.23 walking to the mini. One pad back, the mini walk wins.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
The return adds the rewards up, each shrunk by gamma for how late it comes. A state's value is the return you expect from it under a policy, a fixed way of walking.

The Bellman expectation equation is the one-step look-ahead the screen checks as you walk. The optimal policy depends on gamma.

## What it leaves out
No dice: every step lands where it aims. The lecture counts a pack k steps away as gamma^(k-1); here it is gamma^k, which changes no choice. In rounds 1 to 4 the bot does the walking.

## Log lines
- `bot`: 1 if the bot exists
- `dial`: round, gamma, mini value, mega value, best (0 mini, 1 mega)
- `walked`: round, gamma, best, arrived
- `pad`: policy (0 to mega, 1 to mini), pad, value
- `walk`: pad, its value, value of the pad ahead
- `walkdone`: pads checked, rule held, reached the mega
- `end`: predictions right, made
- `go`: stop number, when F starts stop 6 or 8
