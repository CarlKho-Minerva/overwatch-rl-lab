# Game 3: The Whiteboard
**Lecture 3:** Planning by Dynamic Programming. **You play:** Tracer, planner then diver. **Time:** 5 minutes.

## The idea
Knowing a world's rules (a model), you can find the best plan by arithmetic instead of by dying: every pad looks one step ahead and keeps the best, until nothing changes.

## The Overwatch metaphor
The whiteboard before the round.

## How to play
Played beside the lecture 3 page: read a stop, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits at the same stop for as long as you like. Board: blue start, green goal (+10), two red pits (-10, end the dive), each step -1. Answers: left click, right click, melee.
1. Read the rules, call the first number (08:08): question, F for sweep 1.
2. Watch the good news spread (19:28): question, F for sweep 2.
3. Judge a half-finished board (1:03:45): question, F for sweep 3.
4. Sweep until nothing moves (1:03:45): question, F until 0 pads change.
5. Walk the plan yourself (1:24:03): question, then walk the lines (WASD).
6. Move a pit, then plan again (1:29:03): R on any pad, question, F.
7. Read the debrief (1:36:30).

## What to notice
News moves one pad per sweep. A sweep that changes nothing means done. Midway numbers are no plan's score. The walk earns the start pad's number.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
Planning: the MDP is given. Value iteration repeats the Bellman optimality backup. Lines: greedy policy improvement. Walk: policy evaluation. A sweep costs states times moves, so big worlds defeat it.

## What it leaves out
Moves never slip; no discount. Policy iteration and asynchronous sweeps are not played. The question counts old lines that stopped being a best move, which does not depend on tie-breaking.

## Log lines
- `map`: start, goal, fixed pit, moving pit
- `pre`: sweeps, start and farthest settling sweep, start number, scripted walk
- `sweep`: run, sweep, pads changed, biggest change, start number
- `row`: run, sweep, row, five numbers
- `arrows`: run, row, five moves (0 +x, 1 +z, 2 -x, 3 -z)
- `looks`: sweeps, pads, look-aheads
- `walk`: pads entered, score, start number, end (1 goal, 2 pit, 0 time out 90 s after the first step), restarts
- `pit`: from, to, old lines now wrong, R presses
- `replan`: sweeps, lines flipped
- `RLG,G` (core): a stop begins
