# Game 3: The Whiteboard
**Lecture 3:** Planning by Dynamic Programming. **You play:** Tracer, planner then diver. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
If you know a world's rules (a model), you can find the best plan by arithmetic instead of by dying. Value iteration: every pad looks one step ahead and keeps the best, again and again, until nothing changes.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Drawing the dive on the whiteboard before the round.

## How to play
A 5 by 5 board of pads. Blue ring: start. Green: goal (pays 10). Red: two pits (cost 10, end the dive). Each step costs 1. Pads start at 0.
1. F runs one sweep. Pads that just heard about the goal turn yellow. Press until a sweep changes nothing.
2. Green lines appear: the plan. Walk it (WASD).
3. Stand on a pad, press R: the pit marked THIS PIT MOVES jumps there. F plans again; changed lines turn yellow.
Answers: left click, right click, melee. Six predictions.

## What to notice
News of the goal moves one pad per sweep. The sweep that changes nothing is how you know you are done. Midway numbers are no plan's score. Walking the lines earns your start pad's number.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
Planning: the MDP is given. Value iteration repeats the Bellman optimality backup. The lines are greedy policy improvement; the walk matching the start number is policy evaluation. A sweep costs states times moves look-aheads, so full sweeps fail on big worlds.

## What it leaves out
Moves never slip and there is no discount. Ties go to the lowest move number. Policy iteration and asynchronous sweeps are not played. The seed asked how many arrows flip; the game asks how many old lines stopped being a best move, which does not depend on tie-breaking.

## Log lines
- `map`: start, goal, fixed pit, moving pit
- `pre`: sweeps to settle, start's and farthest pad's settling sweep, start number, scripted walk
- `sweep`: run, sweep, pads changed, biggest change, start number
- `row`: run, sweep, row, five numbers
- `arrows`: run, row, five moves (0 +x, 1 +z, 2 -x, 3 -z, -1 none)
- `looks`: sweeps, pads, look-aheads
- `walk`: pads entered, score, start number, end (1 goal, 2 pit, 0 time), restarts
- `pit`: from, to, old lines now wrong, R presses
- `replan`: sweeps, lines flipped
