# Game 8: Recall
**Lecture 8:** Integrating Learning and Planning. **You play:** Tracer, walker then coach. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
A learner can keep its own copy of what each move did (a model) and replay it between real steps. Replay spreads only what was seen; a stale memory teaches the old world.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Tracer's Recall rewinds her; the replaying bot does Recall on its memory. The moving gap is the enemy rotating: your read on their setup goes stale.

## How to play
Beside the lecture 8 page, stop by stop: read a stop, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits there. WASD walks; clicks and melee answer; F changes replays, R starts or goes on.
1. Walk the maze once yourself (your steps become its memory).
2. Pick replays, then watch race 1.
3. Change the replays, run race 2.
4. Move the gap (R), predict who struggles.
5. Predict the fix, watch race 3.
6. Read the summary (R).

## What to notice
0 replays makes the second bot the plain bot; 5 against 20 barely differs. One bump fixes the old gap; finding the new one takes hundreds of real steps.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
Dyna-Q: a Q-learning update per real step, a model update, then n updates on remembered pairs.

## What it leaves out
A tiny exact maze; no Dyna-Q+ or search. Each race is worked out unseen, then rerun with the same dice, so the answer key is the race you watch. The seed expected the replaying bot to be stuck longer after the move; here the plain bot nearly always is (its values are a stale map too), so it scores the result.

## Log lines
- `seed`: seed, second seed
- `walk`: move (pad x 4 + move), reward, next pad
- `walkend`: steps, bumps, reached goal
- `pick`: race, replays
- `trip`: race, bot (0 plain, 1 replaying), trip, steps
- `firstgoal`: race, bot, trip, pads that know the way
- `know`: plain pads, replaying pads, replays
- `pass`: race, plain unseen and shown, replaying unseen and shown, replays
- `wall`: untrue replays, old gap fixed at step, new gap found at step
- `runs`: replays, races 1 and 2
- `go`: stop where R started it (4 and 6)
- `tie`: race, bot, step, tie set (bit per move), near the tie margin
