# Game 4: Back From Spawn
**Lecture 4:** Model-Free Prediction. **You play:** Tracer, the walker. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
How long until you are back in the fight? You can wait and find out. Or you can fix your guess at every corner, from the leg just walked plus your guess for the rest.

Both learn from experience alone. Only one learns before it arrives.

## The Overwatch metaphor
The walk back from spawn: re-guess your arrival at every corner, or find out when you get there.
Silver's "driving home".

## How to play
Played beside the lecture's page: read a stop, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits at that stop ("STOP k OF 8") as long as you like. WASD walks the rings to the point; left click, right click or melee answers. Mud legs (red) run at 30% speed; the clock runs only between rings. Above each ring, BLUE and ORANGE guess the seconds to the point.

1. Walk the mud leg to ring 1.
2. Finish the walk and watch BLUE.
3. Say which guess leans on a guess.
4. Walk again and compare the jumps.
5. Bet on which guess swings more.
6. Get cut off at ring 2.
7. Ask what a planner would need.
8. Read what the walks showed.

## What to notice
ORANGE changes its mind at ring 1. BLUE waits for the point, then jumps more.

Cut a walk short and only ORANGE learns.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
BLUE is every-visit Monte Carlo, pulled toward the real return. ORANGE is TD(0), pulled toward leg time plus V(next ring): a guess from a guess, called bootstrapping.

Monte Carlo: no bias, more variance. TD: less variance, some bias, and it learns before the end and from unfinished walks. Questions 2 and 5 reach back to lectures 2 and 3.

## What it leaves out
A fixed half step; five walks cannot converge. Mud counts on full walks are a shuffled 1, 2, 4, 5; leg 1 of walk 1 is always mud. No lambda or traces.

## Log lines
- `setup`: step, guess per leg, speeds, legs, walks, cut walk, cut legs
- `counts`: mud legs on full walks 1, 2, 3, 5
- `walk`: walk (0 to 4), mud on legs 1 to 5, legs to walk
- `leg`: walk, leg, mud, seconds
- `td`: walk, ring (0 spawn to 4), old, target, new
- `mc`: walk, ring, old, real time left, new
- `cut`: walk, legs walked
- `swing`: spawn guess total movement, BLUE, ORANGE
- `final`: 0 BLUE or 1 ORANGE, guesses for spawn and rings 1 to 4
