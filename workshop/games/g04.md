# Game 4: Back From Spawn
**Lecture 4:** Model-Free Prediction. **You play:** Tracer, the walker. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
Learn a time from experience alone. One way waits for the end and uses the real total; the other fixes a guess at every step from the time just walked plus its own next guess.

## The Overwatch metaphor
The walk back from spawn to the fight: you re-guess your arrival at every corner, or only find out when you get there. Silver's "driving home" example.

## How to play
WASD walks you from the spawn ring through rings 1 to 4 to the point; left click, right click or melee answers. Abilities are off. Legs are clear (green) or mud (red, 30% speed); the clock runs only between rings. Five walks; walk 4 is cut short at ring 2. Above each ring: BLUE and ORANGE guesses of the seconds to the point, both starting at 3 s a leg.

## What to notice
ORANGE changes at ring 1; BLUE only at the point. BLUE's spawn guess jumps more. Only ORANGE learns from the cut walk.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
BLUE is every-visit Monte Carlo: V(s) moves toward the real return. ORANGE is TD(0): toward the leg time plus V(next ring), which is bootstrapping. Monte Carlo: no bias, more variance. TD: less variance, some bias, learns before the end and from unfinished walks. The five predictions reach back to lecture 2 (the Bellman one-step rule) and lecture 3 (a planner needs the mud odds, a model).

## What it leaves out
Both use a fixed step of one half, and five walks cannot converge. So that walks differ, the mud counts on the four full walks are a shuffled 1, 2, 4, 5, and leg 1 of walk 1 is always mud. No lambda, traces or batch replay.

## Log lines
- `setup`: step, guess per leg, clear %, mud %, legs, walks, cut walk, cut legs
- `counts`: mud legs on full walks 1, 2, 3, 5
- `walk`: walk (0 to 4), mud on legs 1 to 5, legs to walk
- `leg`: walk, leg, mud, seconds
- `td`: walk, ring (0 spawn to 4), old, target, new
- `mc`: walk, ring, old, real time left, new
- `cut`: walk, legs walked
- `swing`: spawn guess total movement, BLUE, ORANGE
- `final`: 0 BLUE or 1 ORANGE, guesses for spawn and rings 1 to 4
