# Game 4: Back From Spawn
**Lecture 4:** Model-Free Prediction. **You play:** Tracer, the walker. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
Learn a time from experience alone: wait for the end and use the real total, or fix a guess at every step from the leg just walked plus your next guess.

## The Overwatch metaphor
The walk back from spawn: re-guess your arrival at every corner, or find out when you get there. Silver's "driving home".

## How to play
Play it beside the lecture's page: read a stop, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits at that stop ("STOP k OF 8") as long as you like. WASD walks the rings to the point; left click, right click or melee answers. Mud legs (red) run at 30% speed; the clock runs only between rings. Above each ring: BLUE and ORANGE guess the seconds to the point.
1. Walk the mud leg to ring 1.
2. Finish the walk and watch BLUE.
3. Say which guess leans on a guess.
4. Walk again and compare the jumps.
5. Bet on which guess swings more.
6. Get cut off at ring 2.
7. Ask what a planner would need.
8. Read what the walks showed.

## What to notice
ORANGE changes at ring 1, BLUE only at the point; BLUE jumps more; only ORANGE learns from the cut walk.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
BLUE is every-visit Monte Carlo (toward the real return). ORANGE is TD(0) (toward leg time plus V(next ring): bootstrapping). Monte Carlo: no bias, more variance. TD: less variance, some bias, learns before the end and from unfinished walks. Questions 2 and 5 reach back to lectures 2 and 3.

## What it leaves out
A fixed half step; five walks cannot converge. Mud counts on full walks are a shuffled 1, 2, 4, 5; leg 1 of walk 1 is always mud. No lambda or traces.

## Log lines
- `setup`: step, guess per leg, speeds, legs, walks, cut walk, cut legs
- `counts`: mud legs on full walks 1, 2, 3, 5
- `walk`: walk (0 to 4), mud on legs 1 to 5, legs to walk
- `leg`: walk, leg, mud, seconds
- `td`: walk, ring (0 spawn to 4), old, target, new
- `mc`: walk, ring, old, real time left, new
- `cut`: walk, legs walked
- `swing`: spawn guess total movement, BLUE, ORANGE
- `final`: 0 BLUE or 1 ORANGE, guesses for spawn and rings 1 to 4
