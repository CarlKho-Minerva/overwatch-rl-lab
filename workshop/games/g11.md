# Game 11: Sombra's Room
**Lecture 11:** The wall she cannot see (partial observability). **You play:** Tracer, who has to walk in. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
Which room Sombra is in is hidden. A ping is evidence, not the answer. You keep a belief, one number moved by one rule after each ping, and decide on it. A ping is worth taking only for what it tells you, and that worth can be computed.

## The Overwatch metaphor
An invisible Sombra waits in one of two rooms. Your ping is Hanzo's sonic arrow, right 8 times in 10. In the last round it is Widowmaker's infra-sight, always right.

## How to play
F pings (costs 1). Walking onto a pad enters that room: empty pays +10, hers costs 30 and hacks you. Five rounds: 1 and 2 guided, with the belief bar hidden until you predict; 3 and 4 free; in 5 the ping is always right.

## What to notice
At 80% entering is worth 2 on average and pinging on 5.71. At 94% one more ping costs more than it gives. Two pings that disagree put you back at 50%. The latest ping alone never tells you the bar.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
A POMDP. The bar is a belief state, Silver's second way to build a state (lecture 1). The plan is value iteration over beliefs. A ping is information gathering, worth most where it could change your action (lecture 9). The same-latest-ping question reaches back to lesson 11's blind runner and lecture 7's look-alike squares.

## What it leaves out
Two rooms, a known accuracy, a Sombra who never moves: the belief is exact. Real hidden states are too many to list. Sombra is an effect, not a bot. In round 2 the question says the bar reads 80% before you decide.

## Log lines
- `plan`: threshold rung, its belief, plan values at 50%, 80%, 94%, perfect ping
- `hide`: round, her room (0 left, 1 right), ping accuracy
- `ping`: round, number, reading, belief before, after, enter value, ping value
- `enter`: round, room, her room, belief, room value, best enter value, ping value, reward, pings
- `voi`: ping gain over entering at 50%, entering gain over pinging at 94%
- `score`: empty rooms, score, predictions right, asked
- `stuck`: round, room (stand-in teleported)
