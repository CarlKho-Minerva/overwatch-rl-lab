# Game 11: Sombra's Room
**Lecture 11:** The wall she cannot see (partial observability). **You play:** Tracer, who has to walk in. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
Sombra is in one of two rooms. You ping, and it says LEFT. Evidence, not the answer: pings can be wrong.

So you keep a belief, one number moved by one rule, and decide on it. A ping is worth only what it tells you, and that worth can be computed.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Your ping is Hanzo's sonic arrow, except this one is right only 8 times in 10.
In the last round it is Widowmaker's infra-sight, always right.

## How to play
Played beside lesson 11's page: read a stop, Alt+Tab, and the game waits at that stop. F pings (costs 1). A pad enters that room: empty pays +10, hers costs 30.

1. Ping once, then say how sure.
2. Ping again, then pick a room.
3. At 80%, walk in or ask again (round 2).
4. Same last ping, different bar (then round 3).
5. Where a ping is worth most (then round 4).
6. One ping that is never wrong (round 5).
7. The debrief, after you press F.

## What to notice
At 80% entering is worth 2 and pinging on 5.71. Eighty percent sure, and the sums say ask again.

At 94% a ping costs more than it gives. Two pings that disagree mean 50%, back where you started.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
A partly hidden world is a POMDP. The bar is a belief state, Silver's second way to build a state (lecture 1), and the plan is value iteration over beliefs.

A ping is information gathering, worth most where it could change your action (lecture 9). Stop 4 reaches back to lesson 11's blind runner and lecture 7's look-alikes.

## What it leaves out
Two rooms, a known accuracy, a Sombra who never moves mid-round: the belief is exact. Sombra is an effect, not a bot. In round 2 the question gives away the 80%. (The real sonic arrow never lies; this one does, on purpose.)

## Log lines
- `plan`: threshold rung, its belief, values at 50%, 80%, 94%, perfect ping
- `hide`: round, her room (0 left, 1 right), ping accuracy
- `ping`: round, number, reading, belief before, after, enter value, ping value
- `enter`: round, room, her room, belief, room value, best enter, ping value, reward, pings
- `voi`: ping gain at 50%, entering gain at 94%
- `score`: empty rooms, score, predictions right, asked
- `stuck`: round, room (stand-in teleported)
- `go`: stop (7, the debrief, started with F)
