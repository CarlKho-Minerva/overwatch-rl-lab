# Game 7: Counter-Pick
**Lecture 7:** Policy Gradient. **You play:** the opponent of a learning bot, picking a hero each round. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
Say a Pharah keeps beating you. You do not score every hero. You pick whatever beat her, a bit more often each time.

This bot does that: it learns odds for its moves directly, without scoring them, and makes what worked more likely.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Counter-picking, against a bot that is quietly learning to counter-pick you.
Here Tracer beats Widowmaker beats Pharah beats Tracer, a rule made for this game, not real Overwatch.

## How to play
Beside the lecture 7 page: read a stop there, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits at that stop (shown beside the title), with no time limit. Each round the bot locks in hidden, then you pick: left click Tracer, right click Widowmaker, melee Pharah. Bars on the right show its odds.

1. Predict what the bars do against one hero.
2. Pick one hero six rounds running and watch its counter climb.
3. Predict where lecture 5's random pick goes.
4. Predict what beats a fixed rule.
5. Predict the mix nobody can exploit.
6. Free play, five rounds, against a fresh bot; your mix is scored.
7. Predict a bigger nudge, then four rounds at 2.5.
8. Debrief.

## What to notice
A third each leaves it nothing to learn.

At nudge 2.5 one lucky round sends a bar near 100%, and it sticks there. Bigger steps do not learn faster. They commit sooner.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
The bars are a softmax policy trained by REINFORCE with a baseline: preference j changes by step size x (reward minus baseline) x (1 if j was picked, else 0, minus its probability). The baseline is a running average.

## What it leaves out
One situation and one-round episodes, so the return is one reward: a bandit, not the island's walk. No critic, no traces. The aliased gridworld is told in one coach line, not played. Seed change: Phase A holds you to the hero you pick first instead of naming one.

## Log lines
- `arena`: dummies made, learner on team 1
- `pick`: round, phase (0 A, 1 B, 2 B big step), your pick, draw k (u = (k + 0.5) / 1000), bot pick, bot reward
- `odds`: round, three odds before, step size, baseline before
- `pref`: round, three preferences after, baseline after
- `mix`: round, your phase B frequencies, exploitability of your mix, of the bot's odds
- `reset`: rounds so far, step size
- `score`: your wins, bot wins, draws, timed-out picks
