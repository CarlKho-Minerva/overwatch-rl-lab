# Game 6: Callouts
**Lecture 6:** Value Function Approximation. **You play:** Tracer, the scout. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
A table keeps one number per state, so it knows nothing about a state it never visited. Describe states by a few features and what you learn in one state spreads to similar ones. That learns fast, and it has a floor.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Callouts. "Mid is hot" covers many exact spots: one sighting tells the team about a whole area. Fast, and sometimes wrong.

## How to play
Played beside the lecture's page: read a stop there, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits at the same stop. WASD walk, F (Interact) scouts or picks a pad, left click, right click or melee answer. Pads show each map's number; the right shows each map's miss.
1. Scout six of twelve pads (each read is off by up to 1).
2. Guess which map wins after six calls: EXACT (12 numbers), THIRDS (3), ONE WORD (1).
3. Predict 60 calls, then press F to bring in 54 more.
4. Find where THIRDS stays wrong, scouting forever.
5. Give one pad its own number on top of THIRDS.
6. The debrief.

## What to notice
After 6 calls THIRDS usually wins; after 60, EXACT. Forever, THIRDS stays at 1.99, mostly from pad 10 (a 9 it calls 3.25). Splitting off pad 10 drops that to 0.55.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
Each map is a linear value function with one-hot group features (state aggregation); its number is the group's average, the least-squares fit. EXACT is the table: one feature per state. The average is lecture 4's Monte Carlo estimate. Choosing the features is most of the work.

## What it leaves out
The mildest form: fixed hand-made groups, plain averages, no gradient steps, no bootstrapping, no learned network, and prediction only, no control. The extra 54 calls visit every pad in turn, not at random. A group with no call guesses 0.

## Log lines
- `truth`: the 12 dangers.
- `see`: call number, pad (0 to 11), noise, reading.
- `miss`: stage (6, 60, 0 = forever), then EXACT, THIRDS, ONE WORD.
- `est`: stage, map (0 to 2, 3 = yours), 12 numbers.
- `pick`: your pad, then your map's miss after 6, 60, forever.
- `go`: 3, when F starts the 54 calls.
