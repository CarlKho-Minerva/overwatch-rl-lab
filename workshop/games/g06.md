# Game 6: Callouts
**Lecture 6:** Value Function Approximation. **You play:** Tracer, the scout. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
A table keeps one number per state, so it knows nothing about a state it never visited. Describe states by a few features and what you learn in one state spreads to similar ones. That learns fast, and it has a floor.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Callouts. "Mid is hot" is three words for many exact spots: one sighting tells the team about a whole area. Fast, and sometimes wrong.

## How to play
WASD walk, F scouts the pad you stand in, left click, right click or melee answer. Twelve pads hide a danger (1 to 9); a read is off by up to 1 and costs a small hit. Round 1: you scout 6 pads, and three maps learn from the same calls: EXACT (12 numbers), THIRDS (3), ONE WORD (1). Round 2: 54 more calls. Round 3: you give one pad its own number on top of THIRDS. Under each pad: each map's number. On the right: each map's typical miss after 6 calls, after 60, and forever.

## What to notice
After 6 calls THIRDS usually wins. After 60, EXACT wins. Forever, THIRDS stays at 1.99, mostly from pad 10, a 9 among 1s and 2s that it calls 3.25. Splitting off pad 10 drops that to 0.55.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
Each map is a linear value function with one-hot group features (state aggregation); its number is the group's average, the least-squares fit. EXACT is the table: one feature per state. The average is lecture 4's Monte Carlo estimate. Choosing the features is most of the work.

## What it leaves out
The mildest form: fixed hand-made groups, plain averages, no gradient steps, no bootstrapping, no learned network, and prediction only, no control. The seed's 60 random calls became 54 that visit every pad in turn, so every pad is seen. A group with no call guesses 0.

## Log lines
- `truth`: the 12 dangers.
- `see`: call number, pad (0 to 11), noise, reading.
- `miss`: stage (6, 60, 0 = forever), then EXACT, THIRDS, ONE WORD.
- `est`: stage, map (0 to 2, 3 = yours), 12 numbers.
- `pick`: your pad, then your map's miss after 6, 60, forever.
