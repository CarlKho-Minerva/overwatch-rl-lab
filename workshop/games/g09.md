# Game 9: Loot Boxes
**Lecture 9:** Exploration and Exploitation. **You play:** Tracer, opening the boxes. **Time:** about 5 minutes, plus reading.

## The idea
Everyone has a main, and a hero they tried twice and dropped. Was dropping it right? You only find out by trying again.

Each open is that choice: the box that has paid best so far, or one that might be better? Regret scores it. Explore where unsure.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Loot boxes with hidden odds and a fixed number of opens: stick to your main, or try a hero that might be better.

## How to play
Beside the lecture's page, stop by stop (g09_stops.json); the game waits at each stop. WASD walks, Interact (F) on a yellow pad opens it, left click, right click or melee answers. Left: you beside three strategies on the same boxes.

1. Open eight boxes.
2. A box at 3 of 4.
3. Greedy or the coin.
4. Blue beams (UCB1 best case) and opens 9 to 16.
5. What the optimist reopens; opens 17 to 24.
6. Replays of 300 opens; opens 25 to 29.
7. The last open.
8. Debrief.

## What to notice
Greedy can lock on to a worse box and never look back. The coin (one random open in ten, lecture 5) pays a tax forever.

The optimist reopens a box it lost on once, because its bonus grows with time. Leave a box alone long enough and it looks interesting again.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
A four-armed Bernoulli bandit. Regret: opens times gap, summed. Greedy and fixed epsilon-greedy have linear regret, growing at a steady pace; UCB1's is logarithmic, always slowing. Information is worth less as opens run out. The counts per box are an information state.

## What it leaves out
Each box has a stack of 30 results drawn at the start; the k-th open of a box gets result k, so all see the same luck. Regret ignores luck. At 300 opens UCB1 still trails the coin; it passes after a few thousand (simulated in the test, not the game).

## Log lines
- `odds`: chance of boxes A to D.
- `draws`, `coin`: the drawn results and coin draws, 15 per line.
- `open`: t, box, which open of it, paid, your total, your regret.
- `step`: t, coin draw, box and pay for greedy, coin, optimist.
- `ub`: t, each box's best case (9 = untried).
- `round`: round, t, totals and regrets.
- `replay`: strategy, checkpoint (100, 200, 300), opens per box over 20 games, average regret. `replaytime`: seconds.
- `end`: totals and regrets, predictions right.
