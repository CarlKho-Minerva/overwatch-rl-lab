# Game 9: Loot Boxes
**Lecture 9:** Exploration and Exploitation. **You play:** Tracer, opening the boxes. **Time:** about 5 minutes.

## The idea
Each open: the box that has paid best so far, or one that might be better? Regret scores it. Explore where unsure.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Loot boxes with hidden odds and a fixed number of opens: sticking to your main, or trying a hero that might be better.

## How to play
WASD walks, Interact (F) on a yellow pad opens it, left click, right click or melee answers. Four rounds of about 8 opens, five predictions. Above each pad: opens and pays. From round 2 a blue beam shows each box's best plausible case from your opens (UCB1: average plus sqrt(2 ln t / n)); untried boxes stand tallest. Left: your total beside three strategies on the same boxes, and regret per round. Right, after question 4: 20 replays of 300 opens each.

## What to notice
Greedy can lock on to a worse box. The coin (one random open in ten, lecture 5) pays a tax forever. The optimist reopens a box it lost on once, because its bonus grows with time.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
A four-armed Bernoulli bandit. Regret: opens times gap, summed. Greedy and fixed epsilon-greedy have linear regret, UCB1 logarithmic. Information is worth less as opens run out. The counts per box are an information state.

## What it leaves out
Each box has a stack of 30 results drawn at the start; whoever opens it for the k-th time gets result k, so all see the same luck. Regret ignores luck. At 300 opens UCB1 still trails the coin; it passes it after a few thousand (simulated in the test, not the game). The game computes its own natural log and checks it.

## Log lines
- `odds`: chance of boxes A to D.
- `draws`: box, half, 15 results. `coin`: half, 15 draws.
- `open`: t, box, which open of it, paid, your total, your regret.
- `step`: t, coin draw, box and pay for greedy, coin, optimist.
- `ub`: t, each box's best case (9 = untried).
- `round`: round, t, your total and regret, each strategy's.
- `replay`: strategy, checkpoint (100, 200, 300), opens per box over 20 games, average regret. `replaytime`: seconds.
- `end`: totals and regrets, predictions right.
