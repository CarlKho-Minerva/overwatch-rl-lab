# Game 1: No Coach
**Lecture 1:** Introduction to Reinforcement Learning. **You play:** Tracer, the learner. **Time:** about 4 minutes.

## The idea
Nobody tells you the rules, so you learn what the score rewards by trying things. Then you act on what you kept from past rounds, not on what you see.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Your first minutes on a new map with no callouts and no coach, only the scoreboard.

## How to play
WASD walks; left click, right click or melee answers. Five white rings stand 13 m around you. The yellow line shows only the last reward and the total. Four rounds:
1. Find out what the score rewards.
2. Same hidden layout. Both totals appear side by side, with the gap.
3. You are held still while you look; the rings vanish, then the clock starts.
4. The rings come back, but the hidden payouts are redrawn and the +50 ring moves.

## What to notice
Each second costs 1. One ring pays +50 and ends the round. One ring sends a -20 bill only when the round ends, so you cannot tell which ring caused it. Round 2 should beat round 1. In round 3 nothing on screen helps you. In round 4 the island looks exactly like round 2.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
No supervisor, only a reward. Feedback can arrive late (the bill), hiding which action earned it. Action, observation, reward, in a loop. The history is every second, ring and bill so far; the state is what you keep of it ("that ring, there"). Going straight to the known ring is exploiting; trying the others is exploring. Round 4 is partial observability: the same view, a different hidden state.

## What it leaves out
You always know where you stand, so your memory is nearly a complete state; real agents see much less. No discount, no value function, no algorithm: you are the learner. Changes from the seed: the trap's cost arrives late (to make delayed feedback felt), round 3 holds you still while the rings vanish (so a quick player cannot reach the ring before they go), and round 4 is added.

## Log lines
- `layout,<round 1 or 4>,<+50 ring>,<billing ring>`
- `touch,<round>,<ring>,<reward now>,<seconds so far>`
- `round,<round>,<reached +50 0/1>,<seconds>,<billed 0/1>,<total>`
- `gap,<round 1 total>,<round 2 total>,<difference>`
- `vanish,3`
