# Game 1: No Coach
**Lecture 1:** Introduction to Reinforcement Learning. **You play:** Tracer, the learner. **Time:** about 4 minutes.

## The idea
Nobody tells you the rules, so you learn what the score rewards by trying things, then act on what you kept from past rounds.

## The Overwatch metaphor
Your first minutes on a new map with no callouts and no coach, only the scoreboard.

## How to play
Played beside the lecture's page, stop by stop: read a stop, Alt+Tab in, and the game waits there ("STOP k OF 7" by the title). WASD walks; left click, right click or melee answers. Five white rings stand 13 m around you; the yellow line shows only the last reward and the total. Each round's clock starts at your first step.
1. Guess what the number does while you stand still.
2. Round 1: find the ring that pays.
3. Will your total still change? The late bill.
4. Round 2, same hidden layout; both totals side by side.
5. Round 3: look, the rings vanish, walk from memory.
6. Round 4: the same view, but the payouts are redrawn.
7. Debrief.

## What to notice
Each second costs 1. One ring pays +50 and ends the round. One sends a -20 bill only when the round ends, so you cannot tell which ring caused it. Round 2 should beat round 1. In round 4 the island looks exactly like round 2.

## The same thing in reinforcement-learning words
No supervisor, only a reward. Late feedback (the bill) hides which action earned it. Action, observation, reward, in a loop. The history is every second, ring and bill so far; the state is what you keep of it ("that ring, there"). Going straight to the known ring is exploiting; trying the others is exploring. Round 4 is partial observability: the same view, a different hidden state.

## What it leaves out
You always know where you stand, so your memory is nearly a complete state. No discount, no value function, no algorithm: you are the learner. Changes from the seed: the trap's cost arrives late, round 3 holds you still while the rings vanish, and round 4 is added.

## Log lines
- `layout,<round 1 or 4>,<+50 ring>,<billing ring>`
- `touch,<round>,<ring>,<reward now>,<seconds so far>`
- `round,<round>,<reached +50 0/1>,<seconds>,<billed 0/1>,<total>`
- `gap,<round 1 total>,<round 2 total>,<difference>`
- `vanish,3`
